Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about our apps and how to reach us.

Where can I get Ankivex apps?

Our apps are made for mobile phones. Write to us at info@ankivextechnologies.com and we'll point you to the right download.

Why does an app ask for permissions?

An app only asks for access it needs for its job. For example, a contacts app needs to read contacts, and a video player needs to read your videos.

I found a bug. What should I do?

Email us with your phone model, the app name and what happened. A screenshot helps. We reply as soon as we can.

Can I suggest a feature?

Yes. Send your idea to info@ankivextechnologies.com. Many of our updates start with a user's suggestion.

Do you work with other businesses?

We're open to partnerships. Email us a short note about your project.
